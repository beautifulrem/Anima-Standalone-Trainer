# Qwen3.5 tokenizer

`tokenizer.json` and `tokenizer_config.json` are unmodified copies from
[`Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B`](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B), revision
`851bf6e806efd8d0a36b00ddf55e13ccb7b8cd0a` (Apache-2.0), as redistributed by
[GumGum10/comfyui-anima-3-8B](https://github.com/GumGum10/comfyui-anima-3-8B).
They are the exact tokenizer files used by the Anima 3.8B ComfyUI node, so
training and inference tokenize prompts identically.
